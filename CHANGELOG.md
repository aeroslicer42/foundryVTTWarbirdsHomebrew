# Changelog

## 0.6.0 — first public beta (2026-10-08)

Companion module for the `warbirds` system: the table's homebrew, generated from its Roll20 handouts and crunch
document.

- Aircraft (Homebrew): 42 airframes with guns, signature systems, traits, hard points and eras.
- Weapons (Homebrew): the crunch document's 20 air-to-air missiles, 56 bombs and air-to-ground missiles, 10 guns and
  gun pods, the aircraft-specific guns and signature systems, and 16 modern personal weapons.
- Gear (Homebrew): modern armour — concealable vests, plate carriers and plates, Gusoku, sneaking suit, helmets.
- Traits (Homebrew): thrust vectoring, COFFIN, pulse engines, EW systems, helicopters, the Modern Day Modifications.
- Manoeuvres (Homebrew): Herbst, Kulbit, Pugachev's Cobra, Hover.
- Rules (Homebrew): the handouts, creation and ordnance notes, and an aircraft catalogue.

Requires the `warbirds` system 0.6.0 or later. Worlds that used the 0.5.x system-bundled packs keep working;
links to the old compendiums are rewritten on first load.
